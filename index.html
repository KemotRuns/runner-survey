Section C
Episode Theme Appeal 各集主題吸引力
For each episode, rate how interested you'd be in watching a 60-minute episode.
請針對以下 8 集主題評分你的觀看意願。
EP01 — When Running Becomes a Global Language / 當跑步成為全世界的語言
International runners discover Taiwan; GPS Art running; "Why Taiwan?" through outsider eyes.
EP02 — The Runner's Body & Science / 跑者的身體與科學
Sports science, training methods, recovery; elite vs. amateur body data; running injuries explained.
EP03 — Breathing at the Foot of the Mountain / 山腳下的呼吸
Taiwan trail running, forest therapy science, indigenous hunting paths, high-altitude potential.
EP04 — The Unseen Runners / 看不見的跑者
Visually-impaired runners and their guide pairs; wheelchair and amputee athletes; the trust that running builds.
EP05 — Running Is a City's Soul / 跑步是一座城市的靈魂
How running revitalizes towns — Tianzhong (rural village), Penghu (island), and the local economy of races.
EP06 — Women's Strides / 女性的腳步
Female empowerment through running; mother-runners, professional women, the Women-Can half-marathon.
EP07 — Running Tribes / 跑者的社群文化
Running team culture, group training, post-run gatherings — the community side of an "individual" sport.
EP08 — Aid Stations as Culture / 補給是一種文化
Taiwan's legendary aid stations: hand-made tangyuan, sticky rice, honey water, squid — local food on the run.
